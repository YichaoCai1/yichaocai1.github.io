## Objective

Next-token prediction rewards representations that support continuation prediction. The question is what internal distinctions that objective requires.

## Structure

Under explicit generative assumptions, next-token prediction can recover latent concept posterior structure, connecting prediction to interpretable concepts.

## Limits

A result for a specified generative process does not establish every form of predictive or action-ready state. One-step prediction leaves further questions about future behavior.

## Design

The ongoing agenda asks which distinctions persist under rollout. Future directions examine how capacity, precision, and action change the state that needs to be preserved.

## Scope

The published concept-posterior result relies on the generative assumptions in I Predict Therefore I Am. The predictive-state, finite-capacity, and action-conditioned questions below are ongoing work or future directions.

## Evidence ipta

Establishes concept-posterior identifiability under explicit assumptions on the data-generating process.
