Yichao Cai

I am a final-year PhD student in Computer Science at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), Adelaide University, advised by Prof. Javen Qinfeng Shi. I received my M.Sc. and B.Eng. degrees from Wuhan University of Technology and spent time as a visiting student researcher at California PATH, UC Berkeley.

Research

My research asks when learning objectives provably identify causal latent structure that supports generalization, and when they fail to do so. I approach this question using identifiability theory, latent-variable modeling, population-level objective analysis, and representation geometry.

Empirically, I study vision-language and autoregressive models, with growing interests in generative modeling and scientific representation learning.
